A look inside
Features
Map

A world map and a globe

Visited, transit, wishlist and planned countries, flight routes, and the places you were born and lived.

Visa

Visa guidance

See which countries are visa-free, visa on arrival or e-visa for your passport, plus a Schengen 90/180 day counter.

Trips

Plan the next one

Boarding-pass countdowns, the weather, public holidays, packing lists and reminders before you fly.

Money

Expenses in any currency

Log spending in the local currency, set a budget, and see what's left per day. Exchange rates update daily.

Flights

Flight log

Every flight with distance and time, airlines, airports and how many times you've gone around the Earth.

Badges

Stamps to collect

Passport-stamp badges with tiers, 47 collections, yearly goals and a Year in review for every year.

Private by design
No tracking

Your travels stay on your phone.

  • No account, no ads, no analytics.
  • Works offline. It goes online only for visa data, exchange rates and the weather.
  • Move to a new phone with a backup file you control, Android or iPhone.
